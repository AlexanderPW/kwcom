What Is the difference between a Career Strategist and a Career Coach and how do I decide which is right for me?

Scope

A career strategy focuses on the broader picture for youir long-term career trajectory, while traditional career coaching aims to address your more immediate needs and specific challenges.

Focus

Creating a career strategy includes a deep diver into your longer-term vision in order to create a strategic roadmap for career success over time. Traditional career coaching focuses on helping you to overcome current obstacles and achieving your short-term goals.

Approach

A career strategist will help you to make strategic decisions and develop action plans on the basis of your long-term goals, desired skill development, and offer advice and strategies that reflect market and industry trends. A career coach will guide and motivate you, helping you to set personal and professional goals, and develop actionable plans to achieve them.
